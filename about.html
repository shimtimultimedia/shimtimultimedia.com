About

Shimti Multimedia is a laboratory for rich media. Since 2007 it has existed to take an idea and give it a form, a character, and a job to do in the world.

About the studio

  • How we got the name

    In Akkadian, bīt šimti is the house where the breath of life was breathed in, and šimtu is the character a thing is given at the moment it is made. A studio is that room, rebuilt as a laboratory.

  • What we do

    Five practices and seventeen disciplines — design, 3D, film, sound, digital — under one roof. AI is why one studio can carry that range at a professional standard rather than an amateur one.

  • How we work

    No two projects have been the same, and the process has never pretended otherwise. A method that bends to the project keeps a hard deadline realistic and a budget honest.

  • Who is behind it

    Founded in 2007 by Bryant Duhart — Stockton, California by way of Germany, working internationally. Every discipline self-taught, which is the method rather than a gap.

    This site is the front door. The output is spread across the places it actually lives, and all of it belongs to the same operation.
