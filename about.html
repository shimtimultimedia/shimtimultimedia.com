About

Shimti Multimedia is a laboratory for rich media. Since 2007 it has existed to take an idea and give it a form, a character, and a job to do in the world.

The name

In Akkadian, bīt šimti was the house where the breath of life was breathed in — the chamber where raw material was given a nature and sent out to work. Šimtu is the word underneath it, and it does not mean luck. It means the particular character a thing is assigned at the moment it is made. A goddess of šimtu was called banat shimti: maker of fate.

That is what a studio does. It takes something with no nature of its own and decides what it will be. Shimti is that house, rebuilt as a laboratory — which is why “The Breath of Life in Multimedia” has been the principle here from the start, and why “Bringing Art To Life” is the same sentence said plainly.

What we make

Eight disciplines, under one roof:

  • Graphic design
  • Logo design
  • Illustration
  • 3D design
  • Animation
  • Videography
  • Video editing
  • Web design

All of it is AI-assisted now, and that is precisely why one studio can carry this range at a professional standard. The tooling has collapsed the distance between disciplines: work that used to need a department, a pipeline and a schedule to match can be done properly by a small team that understands the craft underneath it. The judgement is still human. The speed is not.

How we work

No two projects here have ever been the same, and the process has never pretended otherwise. An agency that builds one universal methodology ends up serving the methodology — the process grows heavier than the work it was meant to carry, and the client pays for that weight twice, once in money and once in time.

We start from the project instead. A process that bends is what keeps a hard deadline realistic and a budget honest, and it is the reason a studio this size can take on work that would otherwise go to a firm three times the cost.
