AI

Every service this studio offers is AI-powered. That is worth saying plainly and then worth explaining, because “we use AI” has stopped meaning anything. What matters is which parts of the work it touches, and which parts it does not.

What it changes

Three things, concretely. Speed: work that took weeks takes days. Scope: a brief that would once have been turned down for want of a department can be taken on. Price: the overhead of that department is not in the quote, because the department is not there. None of that is a discount on quality. It is the removal of a cost that was never doing the client any good.

What stays human

The judgement is still human. The speed is not. Deciding what a thing should be, whether it is finished, and whether it is any good — none of that is handed over. A tool will produce a hundred versions of something and cannot tell you which one is right, because it has no stake in the answer. That is the part you are hiring.

How it is used

Not as a separate service standing beside the others, but as tooling inside all seventeen disciplines — in the modelling, the cutting, the mixing, the code. Which is why there is no “AI version” of a project here at a different price. There is only how the work gets done now. What that range covers is in What we do.

What you can commission

Beyond the work itself, the capability is available on its own.

  • Custom workflows — pipelines built around how your operation actually runs, rather than around how a tool prefers to be used.
  • Automation — the repetitive half of a process, handed to something that will not get bored and start making mistakes at four in the afternoon.
  • Generation — images, audio, video, copy and 3D produced to a brief, at a volume that would not otherwise be affordable.
  • Consultation — working out where this helps and, more usefully, where it does not.

If you are not sure which of those you need, working that out is part of the job. Get in touch.
