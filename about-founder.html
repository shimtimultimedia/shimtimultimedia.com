Who is behind it

Shimti Multimedia was founded in 2007 by Bryant Duhart, who directs the studio and its work.

Where he comes from

Born and raised in Stockton, California. Based in Germany. Working internationally — which is the plain reason the front door greets visitors in thirty-six languages rather than one.

The range

Design, engineering, and the business that carries both, held by one person. That is why the five practices can share a roof without work being handed between departments to reach the end, and it is where the second half of the name comes from: a multi-talent, and so multi in the media.

All of it self-taught — less a gap in the story than the method behind it. None of it was handed over, so none of it is borrowed.

The record

Rather than recite it here, it lives where it belongs: the portfolio.
