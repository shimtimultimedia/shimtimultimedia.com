Contact

One address, straight to the studio. No form, no ticketing system, no autoresponder standing between a question and an answer.

shimtimultimedia@gmail.com

What helps

Nothing formal is required — a sentence is enough to start. If you already know the shape of the thing, these three make the first reply a useful one rather than a list of questions:

  • What you need made
  • Roughly when you need it
  • Roughly what it is worth to you

If you do not know yet, say that instead. Working out what a project actually is counts as part of the work, and it is usually the part that decides whether the rest goes well.
